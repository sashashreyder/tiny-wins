# Research Notes

> General ADHD self-management context for product direction. Not clinical guidance.

## Key themes from reputable sources

### Externalize cognitive load
ADDitude and ADDA recommend getting tasks out of your head via planners, alarms, parking-lot notes, and visual cues — reducing reliance on working memory alone.

**Product implication:** Brain Dump, Open Loops, Distraction Parking Lot, Proof of Progress.

### Break tasks into chunks + immediate rewards
Mary V. Solanto (ADHD Resource Hub, 2024) emphasizes breaking boring/effortful tasks into manageable chunks and rewarding completion of each chunk — a core CBT-for-ADHD strategy.

**Product implication:** Tiny quests, Focus sprints, XP after starting, personal reward shop.

### Small successes over perfection
ADDitude notes crediting yourself for positive actions matters given how much correction ADHD folks often receive.

**Product implication:** Supportive copy, "hard today" bonus, no streak shame, returns metric.

### Sleep & mood as context, not verdict
Track patterns gently without medical claims. Demo sleep insights are placeholders.

**Product implication:** Mood/sleep trackers with "patterns are data" framing.
